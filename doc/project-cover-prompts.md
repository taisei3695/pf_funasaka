# 案件用イメージ画像の生成記録

組み込みの image_gen ツールで作成した概念画像です。実際の制作画面・製品写真を表すものではありません。公開カードと詳細ページに明記します。

保存先: `src/assets/images/project-*-concept.webp`。1440px幅のWebPへ最適化し、ローカルWordPressのメディアにも登録しています。管理画面「制作実績」から差し替えできます。

## 共通プロンプト

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape 16:9 portfolio project cover illustration. Create one sophisticated minimalist editorial 3D still life depicting {SUBJECT}. Consistent art direction for a Japanese developer portfolio: warm off-white backdrop, charcoal and slate blue main objects, tiny muted teal accents, matte paper and ceramic materials, soft studio shadows, refined quiet composition, realistic dimensional lighting. Center the main subject with generous breathing room, suited for cropping to 16:9. No people, logos, brand marks, readable text, letters, numbers, actual customer data, or watermarks. This is an illustrative concept cover, not an actual project screenshot. No collage, no labels, no split panels.
```

## SUBJECT とファイル

- `project-construction-concept.webp`: an architectural scale model of a contemporary Japanese building with a small abstract browser window behind, architectural materials and clean geometric structure
- `project-auction-concept.webp`: curated collectible objects on small display plinths beside an abstract online catalog interface, conveying an auction and product inquiry website
- `project-pest-concept.webp`: a clean minimal Japanese home interior and a small protective translucent glass shield, conveying a pest control service without insects or alarming imagery
- `project-wagashi-concept.webp`: three refined traditional Japanese wagashi sweets on a ceramic tray beside a laptop with abstract product tiles, conveying website maintenance for a confectionery
- `project-studio-concept.webp`: an illustrator's desk with art prints, color swatches, a drawing pen and abstract portfolio frames, conveying a creative portfolio website
- `project-shopify-concept.webp`: an open laptop and a notebook with subtle lesson cards and a small study lamp, conveying an online tutoring and learning website
- `project-agriculture-concept.webp`: miniature agricultural fields and greenhouse arranged beside abstract data panels, conveying cultivation, inventory and agricultural business system development
- `project-manufacturing-concept.webp`: miniature factory production line with geometric parts and abstract data panels, conveying manufacturing workflow and inventory management software
- `project-rally-concept.webp`: a conceptual compact table tennis ball launcher at one end of a table, a small camera sensor, a gently curving dotted ball trajectory; illustration concept rather than a real product photograph
- `project-astronomy-concept.webp`: a tablet showing stylized constellations against a star field and a subtle compass instrument nearby, conveying an orientation-sensor astronomy application
